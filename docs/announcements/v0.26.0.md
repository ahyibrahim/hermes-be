Hermes v0.26.0 is on the wire.

On a phone, tap anywhere on a collapsed side rail to open it. Rotating or widening the screen with a rail open no longer makes it jump. Messages can now have bulleted and numbered lists (start lines with `-`, `*` or `1.`) and ~~strikethrough~~. Under the hood: messages can only be sent while signed in, the web app has its own tests, and a reconnect glitch is fixed.
