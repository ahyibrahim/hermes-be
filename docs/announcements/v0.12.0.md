Hermes v0.12.0 is on the wire.

Create a group with the people who should be in it. The chat fits a phone: both side rails start closed, and opening one closes the other. Send and the other icons sit on their buttons. Short sounds mark send, an unread ping, joining or leaving a call, and mute — the same bell that quiets notifications quiets those cues.

There is still no add-someone-later and no faces in the header. A friend you just invited may need a refresh to see the room.
